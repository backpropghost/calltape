What it does

Quiet to record. Rich to read back.

Silent, both sides

Your mic and the other person, mixed into one clean file. Captured at the audio layer, so there is no beep and nothing announced to them.

Records automatically

Auto-records the moment a call goes live and stops when it ends, hands-free. Cellular and FaceTime arrive through Continuity; WhatsApp is captured directly.

Transcribed and summarized

A speaker-labeled transcript with clickable timestamps, and a short summary, both generated by Apple's on-device models.

Knows who called

Name, number, direction, and contact photo, matched from your call history and Contacts. Search across every transcript.

How it works

It runs. You talk.

1

Detect

CallTape spots the live call and starts recording, hands-free.

2

Capture

It taps the call audio and your mic, mixes them, and files it away.

3

Understand

On-device models turn it into a labeled transcript and summary.

Private by architecture

Nothing leaves the machine.

No account, no server, no analytics. Transcription and summaries run locally, and every recording is a plain file in a folder you choose. CallTape is fully open source, so you can read every line.
